Pip's Sky Islands: Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Pip's Sky Islands is a free learning game for elementary school kids. It is a small family project: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share data.

Who creates the account

Only a parent, guardian or teacher signs in (with Google or an email and password). Children do not create accounts and never type an email. Before any child plays, the grown-up confirms they are the child's parent or guardian (or a teacher with parents' permission) and agrees to this notice.

What we store

  • Grown-up account: email address, the date consent was given, and the 4-digit Grown-ups PIN.
  • Each player: a first name or nickname that the grown-up types, an optional 4-digit player PIN, and game progress (stars, coins, items bought, puzzle statistics, recent mistakes for practice, play minutes per day).
  • Backups: one copy of each player's progress per day, kept for 30 days, so progress can be restored.

We do not collect children's full names, photos, voice, location, contacts, school, or any other personal information. "Read aloud" uses the voices built into your device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data. A copy of the progress is also kept in the browser on devices where the game is played, so it works without internet.

What we do with it

Only to run the game: save progress, show it on any device where you sign in, and show the Grown-ups progress page. No advertising, no profiling, no selling, no sharing with third parties (other than Google Firebase as the storage provider).

Your choices

  • See it: Grown-ups (PIN) shows each player's progress.
  • Download it: Grown-ups → Account and saving → "Download all data".
  • Delete a player: Grown-ups → Players → "Remove this player" (also deletes that player's backups).
  • Delete everything: Grown-ups → "Delete account and all data". This removes the account, all players and all backups.
  • Play without an account: progress then stays only in that browser and nothing is sent to us.

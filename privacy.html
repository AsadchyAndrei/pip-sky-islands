Pip's Sky Islands: Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Pip's Sky Islands is a free learning game for elementary school kids. It is a small family project: no ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share data.

Who creates the account

Only a parent, guardian or teacher signs in (with Google, Apple, or an email and password; with Apple you may choose "Hide My Email", and our emails then go through Apple's relay address). Children do not create accounts and never type an email. Before any child plays, the grown-up confirms they are the child's parent or guardian (or a teacher with parents' permission) and agrees to this notice.

What we store

  • Grown-up account: email address, whether the account belongs to a parent or a teacher (for a teacher, also the name shown to students, such as "Ms. Rivera"), the date consent was given, and the 4-digit parents' PIN.
  • Each player: a first name or nickname that the grown-up types, an optional 4-digit player PIN, and game progress (stars, coins, items bought, puzzle statistics, recent mistakes for practice, play minutes per day).
  • Backups: one copy of each player's progress per day, kept for 30 days, so progress can be restored.
  • Email and notification settings (only if you turn them on): whether you want the weekly email and reminders, your time zone (so they arrive at 5 pm your time), a small weekly summary of each player (minutes per day for the last two weeks, skills mastered, what to practice, the family goal, class tasks), a record of which emails were sent, and, for each device where notifications are on, the browser's notification address.

We do not collect children's full names, photos, voice, location, contacts, school, or any other personal information. "Read aloud" uses the voices built into your device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Classes: what a teacher sees

A grown-up can choose to join a child to a teacher's class by typing the teacher's 6-character class code. The teacher then sees a progress summary for that child: the first name or nickname, which skills are mastered, stars per island, puzzles answered, minutes played in the last 7 days, which class tasks are finished, and up to 15 recent mistakes (the question, the child's answer and the right answer). The teacher never sees the grown-up's email, other children, or anything else in the account. The grown-up can leave the class at any time (Parents → Progress → "Leave the class"), and the teacher can remove the child; either way the summary is deleted or no longer updated.

For teachers

  • Turning on teacher tools, you confirm that you are a teacher and that your school allows you to use this app with your students, or that you will get their parents' permission first. Under COPPA, a school may give this permission for use only in the school's educational context.
  • Add students by first name or nickname only. Students on your classroom devices are stored in your account, like a family's players; they log in with two pictures that you print on login cards.
  • A class holds its name, your display name (for example "Ms. Rivera"), the grade, the class code, the topics you mark as "in class now" and the tasks you send. Anyone signed in who has the class code can see these, so do not put student information in them.
  • You can download everything (Parents → Account and saving → "Download all data"), remove a student, or delete a class with all its summaries. Deleting your account deletes your classes too.
  • Co-teachers: you can invite another teacher with a code. A co-teacher sees the same progress summaries as you and can send tasks, lessons and notes; only you manage students, codes and the class. You can remove a co-teacher at any time.
  • Google Classroom (optional): to fill in a class list, you can sign in with Google and allow Pip to read your Classroom class names and student names. This is read once in your browser, used only to fill the "Add students" box (as first name and last initial), and never stored or sent to our servers. Pip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. "Share to Classroom" links simply open Classroom with a text you can edit.
  • Class quest: a shared goal adds up the class's right answers or minutes on our server. Students see only the class total and their own share, never other children's names or scores.
  • Student data is used only to run the game and the teacher desk: no ads, no profiling, never sold or shared.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging for notifications). Weekly emails and reminders are sent through Resend, an email delivery service, which receives only the email address and the email's text. Security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data; a class's progress summaries can be read only by that class's teacher and by each child's own grown-up. A copy of the progress is also kept in the browser on devices where the game is played, so it works without internet.

Pip's voice: to read questions aloud in Pip's own voice, the game sends the text of the question or of Pip's line to our server, which makes the audio with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech and keeps it so the same sentence plays at once next time. Names are removed from the text on the device before it is sent, and nothing about who is playing is sent with it. Parents can pick a device voice instead in Parents' Corner → Settings; then nothing is sent.

What we do with it

Only to run the game: save progress, show it on any device where you sign in, show the Parents' Corner progress page, and, if you turn them on, send the weekly email, reminders and notifications. No advertising, no profiling, no selling, no sharing with third parties (other than Google Firebase as the storage and server provider and Resend for sending emails you asked for).

Your choices

  • See it: Parents (PIN) shows each player's progress.
  • Download it: Parents → Account and saving → "Download all data".
  • Delete a player: Parents → Players → "Remove this player" (also deletes that player's backups).
  • Delete everything: Parents → "Delete account and all data". This removes the account, all players and all backups.
  • Emails and notifications: off unless you turn them on in Parents → Account → "Emails and notifications"; every email has a link to turn them off.
  • Challenge a friend: a challenge link holds only the puzzles, the score and, if the child leaves the box checked, their first name or nickname. It is not stored by us; the family decides whom to send it to.
  • Play without an account: progress then stays only in that browser and nothing is sent to us.

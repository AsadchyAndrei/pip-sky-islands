Set up your class in 2 minutes

Want a look first? Open the teacher demo: a sample class with a week of progress. No sign-up.

  1. Open pipskyislands.com and sign in 30 secTap the cloud button at the top and sign in with Google or an email. Choose Teacher and type the name students see, like "Ms. Rivera".
  2. Make a class 20 secOn your teacher desk: a class name and a grade. You get a 6-character class code.
  3. Invite the families 30 secFill in your name and the code in the letter below and send it (email, ClassDojo, Google Classroom, or print it). Families who already play at home join with the code, and their child's progress comes with them.
  4. Optional: play at school too 40 secIn the class, open Students: type or paste first names (or import from Google Classroom), print login cards, and tap Use this device for the class on a Chromebook or iPad. Kids sign in with their name and two pictures.

That's it. From the next day the Progress tab shows who played and where the class needs help.

What you will see

Your teacher desk, one class at a time. Everything is by skill and standard code.

  • Who needs whatStudents grouped into needs help, on track and ahead, with one-click practice for each group.
  • Class heatmapEvery island and skill for every student, and "where the class needs help".
  • By standardResults by CA standard code, plus CSV downloads for your gradebook.
  • Tasks and lessonsMark what you teach right now, send tasks with a due date, or run a 10–30 minute lesson with a live view.
  • Messages and reportsA short note to families, printable family reports for conferences, and a weekly class email.
  • Co-teachersInvite a specialist or aide with a code. They see progress and send tasks; you keep the class.

Good to know

  • Free for families and teachers. No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing sold or shared.
  • You see only a child's first name and learning progress: skills, stars, minutes this week, finished tasks and recent mistakes. Nothing else from the family's account.
  • About 15 minutes a day is plenty. Pip's Pick chooses what each child should practice next.
  • Works in any browser, on Chromebooks, iPads and phones, and keeps working offline.
  • English learners: turn on Spanish help in the class settings. In math and science students get a "Pista en español" button and can tap key words for their Spanish meaning; reading and grammar stay in English. Every Spanish line is open for a bilingual colleague to check.
